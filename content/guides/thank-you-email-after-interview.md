You made it through the interview. Before you collapse, there's one more step that takes five minutes and can make a real difference: the thank-you email.

A thoughtful thank-you note shows professionalism, reinforces your interest, and gives you one more chance to remind the interviewer why you're a good fit.

## Does a thank-you email really matter?

It rarely wins a job on its own, but it's an easy way to stand out, and not sending one can occasionally hurt you, especially in close decisions. Many students skip it. Sending a specific, sincere note puts you in the group that didn't.

## When to send it

- **Within 24 hours** of the interview, ideally the same day.
- **To each person who interviewed you**, if you have their emails.
- **By email**, not a handwritten card, unless you know the company values that tradition. Email arrives before decisions are made.

If you don't have an interviewer's email, ask the recruiter to pass along your thanks, or send a short LinkedIn message. Here's [how to find the recruiter or interviewer's contact details](/guides/how-to-find-the-recruiter-for-a-job).

## What a great thank-you email includes

1. **A clear subject line**: "Thank you for today's conversation."
2. **A genuine thank-you** for their time.
3. **One specific detail from the conversation**, which proves you were paying attention.
4. **A brief reminder of your fit**, in one sentence.
5. **A friendly close.**

Keep it under 150 words.

## Template 1: After a first-round or phone interview

> **Subject:** Thank you for today's conversation
>
> Hi Sarah,
>
> Thank you for taking the time to talk with me today about the Marketing Internship. I really enjoyed hearing about the campus ambassador program you're launching this spring, and it made me even more excited about the role.
>
> My experience growing our club's Instagram audience feels like a strong fit for that kind of student-focused content, and I'd love to help build it.
>
> Thanks again, and I look forward to hearing about next steps.
>
> Best,
> Jordan Lee

## Template 2: After a panel interview

Send a separate, slightly different note to each person. Reference something each one said.

> **Subject:** Thank you for meeting with me
>
> Hi Marcus,
>
> Thank you for being part of my interview today for the Data Analyst Internship. Your description of how the team rebuilt its forecasting dashboard was fascinating, and it's exactly the kind of project I'd love to contribute to.
>
> I appreciated the chance to learn more about the team, and I'm very interested in the role.
>
> Best regards,
> Priya Shah

## Template 3: After a final-round interview

> **Subject:** Thank you, and excited about the opportunity
>
> Hi Dana,
>
> Thank you for spending time with me today and introducing me to the rest of the team. After our conversation about how interns own a real project from start to finish, I'm even more certain this is where I want to spend my summer.
>
> Please let me know if there's anything else I can provide. I'm looking forward to hearing from you.
>
> Thank you again,
> Alex Kim

## Template 4: Following up if you haven't heard back

If the date they gave for a decision passes with no word, it's fine to follow up politely:

> **Subject:** Following up on the Marketing Internship
>
> Hi Sarah,
>
> I hope you're doing well. I wanted to follow up on my interview for the Marketing Internship on March 3. I'm still very excited about the role and would love to hear about any updates on the timeline.
>
> Thank you,
> Jordan Lee

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Sending the same generic note to everyone.** Panelists sometimes compare.
- **Writing too much.** It's a thank-you, not a second cover letter.
- **Rehashing a weak answer at length.** If you want to add something, one sentence is enough.
- **Typos and wrong names.** Double-check spelling, especially names.
- **Waiting days to send it.** Decisions can happen quickly.

## Frequently asked questions

### Should I send a thank-you email after an internship interview?

Yes. A short, specific thank-you within 24 hours is professional and helps you stand out from candidates who don't send one.

### How soon should I send a thank-you email after an interview?

Within 24 hours, ideally the same day.

### Should I send a thank-you to every interviewer?

Yes, if you have their contact information. Personalize each note with something specific from your conversation.

### What if I don't have the interviewer's email?

Ask the recruiter to pass along your thanks, or send a short message on LinkedIn.
