# Direct Competitors to Unnepo: Student and Early-Career Job Application Tools (as of Nov 2026)

Method note: Chrome Web Store (CWS) figures for Simplify, Jobright, Huntr, and Careerflow were read directly from live listings on 2026-11-23. Most pricing and funding figures come from third-party review blogs, many run by competing auto-apply vendors, so they are flagged as such. Vendor pricing pages could not be fetched directly. User counts are almost all self-reported.

## Simplify (Simplify Copilot / Simplify+): features, pricing, users, funding, monetization, growth

### Takeaway
Simplify is the category leader for students and the closest competitor to Unnepo. It has about 500K CWS users with a 4.8 rating, and a GitHub internship list with about 48.5K stars serves as its free distribution engine. It gives away autofill and tracking, and charges $39.99/mo for Simplify+ (AI tailoring, cover letters, answers, AI email, Autopilot). Funding is modest (about $3-4.35M, YC W21, no Series A reported).

### Cited Findings
- CWS listing "Simplify Copilot - Autofill job applications, job tracker & AI resumes": 500,000 users, 4.8/5 from 3.9K ratings, updated Nov 13 2026, v3.1.15. Features: autofill on Workday/Lever/Greenhouse and other major ATSs, keyword-gap match vs. resume, AI resumes and cover letters, job recommendations by preferences (salary, location), tracker for saved and submitted jobs and interview notes. — [CWS listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/simplify-copilot-autofill/pbanhockgagggenencehbnadejlgchfc)
- Free: Copilot autofill, job tracker, Gmail integration, manual resume builder. Paid Simplify+: AI resume tailoring, cover letters, application answers, AI email, Autopilot (auto-submit). — [sprad.io review](https://sprad.io/blog/simplify-jobs-review)
- Simplify+ pricing: $19.99/week, $39.99/month, $89.99 for 3 months (marked "Most Popular"). No money-back guarantee. Vendor claims "2x more interviews" and "5+ hours saved every week". — [jobhire.ai review (competitor vendor)](https://jobhire.ai/blog/simplify-jobs-review); same tiers in [sprad.io](https://sprad.io/blog/simplify-jobs-review)
- Reviews note Simplify+ credits expire monthly and the job board can run thin. — [sprad.io](https://sprad.io/blog/simplify-jobs-review)
- Users (self-reported): 1.8M+ candidates and 300M+ applications submitted via Copilot. The pricing page says "50k+ Simplify+ users", which would be a paid-subscriber signal, but the page uses the figure inconsistently. — [jobhire.ai](https://jobhire.ai/blog/simplify-jobs-review). Other sources cite 1.5M+. Simplify's own 2024 post said "nearly half a million". — [sprad.io](https://sprad.io/blog/simplify-jobs-review)
- Funding: YC W21. $3M seed led by Craft Ventures (Feb 2024). About $4.35M total, no Series A as of mid-2026. One aggregator shows $3.2M across 3 rounds, so the figures conflict. — [sprad.io](https://sprad.io/blog/simplify-jobs-review); [Yespress profile](https://yespress.io/simplify)
- Monetization: Simplify+ subscriptions plus revenue from "employers who hire through us" (recruiting side). — [sprad.io](https://sprad.io/blog/simplify-jobs-review)
- Growth engine: the GitHub repo SimplifyJobs/Summer2026-Internships has 48.5K stars, 3.3K forks, and 1.8K watchers, and is "Updated daily by Simplify and Pitt CSC". Simplify promotion in the repo is limited to attribution. — [GitHub](https://github.com/SimplifyJobs/Summer2026-Internships)
- Additional features advertised (per a competitor page): job recommendations, resume scoring, AI tailoring, Copilot autofill, tracking, Autopilot, Network Copilot. Trustpilot rating is 2.8 from 21 reviews (checked Oct 21 2026). — [Jobright compare page (competitor)](https://jobright.ai/compare/simplify)
- Reddit complaints (as quoted by a competitor's review): r/csMajors "it feels like glorified autofill to me" (302 upvotes), with the same user saying it "doesnt even work half the time". Another user reports a 0% hit rate on about 100 apps over 3 months (Jan 2026). A third asks whether mass-applying saves or wastes time. Praise: one user edited their complaint to say the rebuilt extension is "night and day different" after Simplify acknowledged it was bloated. The review also notes that people install the extension expecting auto-apply and get assisted autofill. — [jobhire.ai](https://jobhire.ai/blog/simplify-jobs-review)

### Inferences
- Simplify has already made autofill and tracking free for students, so Unnepo cannot charge for basic autofill. Unnepo's free tier caps autofills at 3/day, while Simplify autofill is unlimited and free. That puts Unnepo at a disadvantage on the most-used feature.
- Simplify+ at $39.99/mo sits above Unnepo's $29.99/mo. Unnepo's $100/semester (about $25/mo over 4 months) undercuts Simplify's $89.99/3 months only slightly per month, but it matches the academic calendar better.
- If "50k+ Simplify+ users" are paying subscribers at a $30-40 average, Simplify's paid revenue could be in the low millions per month. This is unverified and the page is ambiguous.
- The GitHub list is a CS-heavy channel that Unnepo cannot easily copy. Non-CS majors (business, engineering, healthcare, government, liberal arts) are less well served by it.

### Gaps
- No independently verified revenue, paying-subscriber count, or 2026 funding round for Simplify.
- Direct Reddit threads were not fetched. The Reddit quotes are secondhand via a competing vendor's blog.
- Whether Simplify currently surfaces verified recruiter emails (vs. "Network Copilot" referral suggestions) was not confirmed.

## Jobright.ai: features (agent, insider connections), pricing, traction

### Takeaway
Jobright is the most aggressive AI-agent competitor. It combines job matching, resume tailoring, autofill, insider connections, and an "Agent", with a free tier that has daily caps similar to Unnepo's. Turbo costs about $39.99/mo. Its CWS footprint (300K users, 4.9 from 434 ratings) is smaller than Simplify's, but it claims 2-3M platform users and has strong Trustpilot numbers.

### Cited Findings
- CWS "Jobright Autofill – Instant Job Applications, Job Match, AI Tailor Resume": 300,000 users, 4.9/5 from 434 ratings, updated Nov 21 2026, v1.27.0. The listing text separately claims "500K+ job seekers". Features: one-click ATS autofill, keyword match and ATS score, AI resume builder, AI job matching, tracker. Has in-app purchases. — [CWS listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/jobright-autofill-%E2%80%93-insta/odcnpipkhjegpefkfplmedhmkmmhmoko)
- Free tier has daily limits, reported as about 2 tailored resumes, 4 autofills, and 2 cover letters per day. Turbo is $39.99/mo, $17.99/week, or about $89.99/quarter. There is no public pricing page. The monthly price was reportedly raised from $29.99 earlier in 2026. One outlier lists $19.99/mo and another says "from $34.99/month". — aggregated from [sprad.io](https://sprad.io/blog/jobright-review), [careery.pro](https://careery.pro/blog/ai-job-search/jobright-ai-review), [jobsaicopilot](https://jobsaicopilot.com/jobright-review/)
- Features per Jobright's own compare page (updated Oct 21 2026): Jobright Agent workflows, Insider Connections (finds contacts at target companies, no guarantee of referral), extension autofill on "supported application sites", Match Score, "Orion" fit explanations, resume tailoring, personalized job matching. Positioning line: "Simplify fills forms. Jobright finds the right jobs, tailors your resume, writes your cover letter, surfaces insider referrals, and applies for you." — [Jobright compare](https://jobright.ai/compare/simplify)
- Trustpilot is 4.8 from 3,652 reviews (Oct 21 2026, as reported by Jobright). — [Jobright compare](https://jobright.ai/compare/simplify)
- Jobright claims its autofill works on 90% of major ATS platforms, including Workday and Greenhouse. — [search summary of jobright.ai/compare](https://jobright.ai/compare/simplify)
- Users (self-reported, conflicting): 520K+ registered, 2M (homepage Aug 2026), 3M (homepage Oct 2026). — [sprad.io](https://sprad.io/blog/jobright-review); [careery.pro](https://careery.pro/blog/ai-job-search/jobright-ai-review)
- Funding (conflicting): about $7.7M across 2 rounds including Indeed's investment arm. A $4.5M round led by Lanchi Venture is reported via TechCrunch. $3.2M (2025) is reported via The Register. HQ is Santa Clara, CA. — [sprad.io](https://sprad.io/blog/jobright-review); [favtutor](https://favtutor.com/jobright-ai-review/)

### Inferences
- Jobright's free-tier caps (about 4 autofills/day) look a lot like Unnepo's (3/day), so Unnepo's limits are in line with market practice. Unnepo's $29.99 is now $10 below Jobright Turbo after Jobright's reported price increase.
- "Insider Connections" overlaps directly with Unnepo's people/verified-emails feature. Unnepo's difference has to come from verified emails plus written follow-ups plus reminders, not from contact discovery alone.
- Jobright is generalist (all job seekers). It is not student- or semester-specific.

### Gaps
- Jobright's official pricing page is not public, so tiers come from third-party reviews only.
- No independently verified revenue. Funding figures conflict.
- What the "Agent" actually automates (auto-submit or not) is not clearly documented.

## Handshake: native student offering, threat or complement

### Takeaway
Handshake (18M+ students and alumni, 1,500+ schools) is now financially dominated by its AI-training/data-labeling business (about $1B gross annualized). Its student-side AI is modest: the Sidekick resume helper (launched June 2026), resume-to-profile autofill, and AI job collections. It does not autofill external ATS forms or track off-platform applications, so for now it complements Unnepo more than it threatens it. The main risk is that it owns student attention and the channel to career centers.

### Cited Findings
- 18M+ students and alumni, 950K+ employers, 1,500+ universities. Last priced at a $3.5B valuation (Series F, 2022, Coatue). CB Insights lists $3.3B. — [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/handshake/); [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/handshake)
- AI training gross annualized revenue is nearly $1B, up from $550M in Jan 2026. Total gross is about $1.1B (Apr 2026), of which the legacy recruiting business is about $150M. Net revenue is about $450M after contractor payouts (Sacra estimate, Apr 2026). Handshake acquired Cleanlab in Feb 2026. These are third-party estimates. — [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/news/127345-handshakes-arr-crosses-1b-as-ai-training-revenue-surges/); [Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/handshake-indeed-for-data-labelers/)
- Student AI features: "Sidekick" AI resume tool (beta Apr 15 2026, launched June 17 2026). Resume-to-profile autofill suggestions (rolling out July 21 2026). AI Fellow and AI project builder badges. AI-generated job collections tested from Oct 2025. Redesigned iOS application flow and "Employer visible" resume uploads (May 29 2026). — [Handshake product updates](https://updates.joinhandshake.com/announcements/handshake-student-product-updates-june-5-2026); [Syracuse Handshake updates](https://answers.atlassian.syr.edu/wiki/spaces/Handshake/pages/524222467/Updates+10+16+2025)
- Employer-side AI direct messaging (Aug 2025) and AI applicant management (Sep 2025) target recruiters, not students. — [Handshake updates](https://updates.joinhandshake.com/announcements/ann_S8UDtdj6a3Rmc)
- Students can message recruiters via a "Send a message" link on postings (undated university page). — [UNL newsroom](https://newsroom.unl.edu/announce/parentnews/13763/78565)
- Handshake gave students a Google AI Plus offer in summer 2026. It has ended. — [Handshake blog](https://joinhandshake.com/blog/students/handshake-google-ai-plus-2026/)
- The Handshake AI Fellowship pays students and grads for AI-training work. Reported pay ranges conflict ($25-$155/hr). — [Handshake support](https://support.joinhandshake.com/hc/en-us/articles/32244754462615-Apply-to-the-Handshake-AI-Fellowship); [aitraining.jobs](https://aitraining.jobs/platforms/handshake-ai)

### Inferences
- Handshake's growth and management focus has shifted to AI data labeling. A cross-ATS student autofill or tracker product from Handshake seems unlikely in the near term (inference, not confirmed).
- Many Handshake postings redirect to Workday or Greenhouse, where Unnepo's autofill and auto-tracking apply. Unnepo works alongside Handshake rather than against it.
- Handshake's "Sidekick" resume help is free, so basic AI resume feedback is being commoditized for students at the platform layer.

### Gaps
- The full list of student AI features is incomplete because Handshake's support article returned 403.
- No evidence was found of Handshake offering student AI cover letters, ATS autofill, or follow-up email features.

## Teal, Huntr, Careerflow, Wonsulting, Prentus, Otta/WTTJ, and other extensions

### Takeaway
Teal and Huntr are tracker-first tools priced at $29-40/mo and aimed at general and mid-career job seekers. Teal has no ATS autofill. Huntr added autofill but has a small CWS base (90K). Careerflow (200K CWS, 4.3) is LinkedIn-optimization plus tracker with AI behind a Premium tier. Wonsulting, Prentus, and Otta/WTTJ have little verifiable 2026 student pricing or traction data.

### Cited Findings
- **Teal**: CWS extension (ID opafjjlpbiaicbbgifbejoochmmeikep, renamed "Teal - Job Search Companion") is rated 4.9 from about 3.1K ratings, with about 200K users per one snapshot. Teal's own site claims 140K+ users. It was a Chrome Web Store "Favorite of 2023". It works on 40+ job boards. — [CWS reviews](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/teal-free-job-search-cont/opafjjlpbiaicbbgifbejoochmmeikep/reviews); [Teal](https://tealhq.com/post/teal-review)
  - Teal+ costs $13/week, $29/month, or $79/quarter, with no annual plan (one source says $9/week). The free plan caps keyword matching at the top 5 keywords. Teal has no autofill and no auto-apply. — [makerstack review](https://makerstack.co/reviews/teal-review/); [toolquestor](https://toolquestor.com/vs/huntr-vs-teal); [resumly roundup](https://www.resumly.ai/best/best-job-search-chrome-extensions)
- **Huntr**: CWS has 90,000 users, 4.6/5 from about 1.3K ratings, last updated Apr 27 2026 (v2.0.45). Features: save jobs from boards and ATSs, profile autofill with "one-click quick apply", kanban tracker, per-job to-dos, notes, contacts, map view, unemployment-claim activity log, shared boards, analytics. — [CWS listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/huntr-job-search-tracker/mihdfbecejheednfigjpdacgeilhlmnf?hl=en)
  - Pro costs $40/mo monthly, $30/mo quarterly ($90), or $26.66/mo biannually ($160). The free plan allows 100 tracked jobs and 2 tailored resumes (pricing page snapshot Sep 28 2026). — [toolquestor / jobmentis](https://www.jobmentis.com/en/job-search-tools-cost); [toolquestor](https://toolquestor.com/vs/teal-vs-huntr)
  - Huntr's unemployment-claim logging feature signals a laid-off, mid-career audience rather than students (inference from listing).
- **Careerflow**: CWS has 200,000 users, 4.3/5 from 288 ratings, updated Sep 24 2026 (v2.20.9). Free: LinkedIn profile score/optimizer and a tracker that saves from 45+ platforms (kanban, notes, to-dos). Premium: AI cover letters on 20+ sites, LinkedIn post and headline generator, JD summarizer, plus autofill. — [CWS listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/careerflow-ai-job-applica/iadokddofjgcgjpjlfhngclhpmaelnli). Pricing is "from $8.99/mo". Details are thin. — [jobmentis](https://www.jobmentis.com/en/job-search-tools-cost)
- **Wonsulting / WonsultingAI**: freemium with a free-forever plan. The starting paid price is not disclosed. — [SpotSaaS](https://www.spotsaas.com/product/wonsulting-ai/pricing)
- **Prentus**: positioned for "your first job after completing a degree, bootcamp, or certificate". Sells to higher-ed institutions (B2B2C) with career-coach office hours. No student prices are shown. — [Prentus for higher ed](https://prentus.com/pricing/for-higher-ed)
- **Otta / Welcome to the Jungle**: Otta is rebranding into Welcome to the Jungle. It is employer-paid ("contact vendor") and free to candidates. No student-specific product was found. — [WTTJ employers](https://employers.welcometothejungle.com/pricing); [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/otta/pricing)
- Other extensions with usage include JobFill ("Autofill Smartly") and a long tail of auto-apply tools (AIApply, FastApply, JobHire.ai, Wobo) that publish SEO comparison content. — [CWS JobFill](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/autofill-smartly-jobfill/kbgfilncepjeoodogmebahnloidgaibg); [fastapply roundup](https://blog.fastapply.co/best-auto-apply-chrome-extensions-2026)

### Inferences
- The $29-40/mo price band is standard (Teal $29, Huntr $40, Simplify $39.99, Jobright $39.99). Unnepo's $29.99 is at the low end, and $100/semester is distinctive. No competitor found offers semester billing.
- Quarterly plans (Simplify $89.99, Jobright about $89.99, Huntr $90, Teal $79) are the closest analog to Unnepo's semester plan. Unnepo's $100 covers about 4 months, so it is roughly price-competitive.
- Prentus's B2B sale to universities is an alternative distribution route (career centers) that none of the consumer extensions appear to pursue.

### Gaps
- Teal funding and revenue were not researched this round. My earlier knowledge says Teal raised a Series A (pre-2025) but no verified figures were collected.
- No reliable 2026 data was found on Huntr or Careerflow funding or revenue, Wonsulting traction, Prentus pricing, or Otta student usage.

## Commoditized vs. monetizable features; white space for a student tool

### Takeaway
Free everywhere: ATS autofill, job tracking, job boards, keyword match, basic resume feedback. Paid everywhere: AI tailoring, cover letters, short answers, AI email, and auto-apply. White space for Unnepo: (a) automatic tracking from confirmation pages without manual saving, (b) verified contacts plus written follow-ups plus reminders tied to each application, (c) student-calendar pricing and positioning, (d) non-CS majors and non-GitHub channels, and (e) trust (the student always presses Submit) as a counter to auto-apply skepticism.

### Cited Findings
- Simplify gives free autofill, tracker, Gmail integration, and resume builder. AI writing and Autopilot are paid. — [sprad.io](https://sprad.io/blog/simplify-jobs-review)
- Teal and Huntr give free trackers with caps. AI tailoring is behind $29-40/mo. — [toolquestor](https://toolquestor.com/vs/teal-vs-huntr)
- Careerflow gives a free tracker and LinkedIn optimizer. AI cover letters are Premium. — [CWS](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/careerflow-ai-job-applica/iadokddofjgcgjpjlfhngclhpmaelnli)
- Handshake gives free AI resume help (Sidekick). — [Handshake updates](https://updates.joinhandshake.com/announcements/handshake-student-product-updates-june-5-2026)
- Jobright paywalls beyond daily caps on autofill, tailoring, and cover letters. — [careery.pro](https://careery.pro/blog/ai-job-search/jobright-ai-review)
- User skepticism of volume tools includes "glorified autofill", 0% hit rates after 100 apps, and disappointment that tools don't auto-apply. — [jobhire.ai](https://jobhire.ai/blog/simplify-jobs-review)
- Jobright's Insider Connections explicitly do not guarantee a referral. — [Jobright compare](https://jobright.ai/compare/simplify)

### Inferences
- Charging per-day caps on autofill (Unnepo free = 3/day) fights a free baseline set by Simplify (unlimited) and Huntr. Unnepo should consider making autofill unlimited and metering only AI generation and contact or email features.
- The most defensible paid wedge is outreach that leads to interviews: verified emails, follow-up drafting, and reminders. Students' main complaint is volume without responses, and the competitors' versions (Simplify Network Copilot, Jobright Insider Connections) are suggestion-level.
- Semester pricing ($100) is unique among the competitors found and matches student budgets and recruiting cycles. It is worth leading with in marketing.
- Distribution white space: Simplify owns CS via GitHub lists, and Handshake owns career centers. Business, finance, engineering, and government or nonprofit internships (Workday/Deltek-heavy) are less owned, and Unnepo's SEO guides target general students.
- A Handshake partnership or integration risk is low in the short term, given Handshake's AI-labeling focus.

### Gaps
- No quantitative data on conversion rates (free to paid) for any competitor.
- No primary Reddit thread analysis was done (fetch budget). Sentiment comes from secondary, vendor-authored sources.
- No data on student willingness to pay vs. general job seekers.
